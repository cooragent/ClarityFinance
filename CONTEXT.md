# Clarity Finance

Clarity Finance separates shared market research from each user's private simulated investing activity.

## Language

**User Account**:
The identity that owns private preferences, research profiles, and one simulation account.
_Avoid_: Portfolio account, trading account

**Simulation Account**:
A user's virtual-capital ledger used only for simulated stock transactions.
_Avoid_: User account, real account

**Simulated Position**:
A stock quantity and cost basis owned by a simulation account, with no claim on real securities.
_Avoid_: Real position, my holdings

**Public Portfolio**:
A shared disclosure of an investor's or fund's reported holdings; it is research data until followed.
_Avoid_: User portfolio

**Follow Allocation**:
A one-time simulated-capital allocation that copies a public portfolio's reported weights into a user's simulation account.
_Avoid_: Followed holding, real investment
